# Maintainers

Current maintainer:

- The owner of `crewplaneai/crewplane`.

## Maintainer duties

- triage new issues;
- review pull requests;
- keep CI green;
- publish releases;
- respond to security reports;
- update roadmap and docs;
- maintain repository automation.

## Adding maintainers

Trusted contributors may become maintainers after repeated high-quality contributions, security-conscious behavior, and alignment with the project direction.
